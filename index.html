Wagering Requirement refers to the total betting amount you must reach before your bonus can be converted into withdrawable balance.
For example: if a promotion requires a 10x wagering multiplier and you receive a 1,000 bonus, you must wager a total of "(Deposit Amount + 1,000) × 10" before you can withdraw.

Please note: Wagering does not mean losing — as long as your bet is settled, it counts toward the wagering requirement regardless of win or loss.
